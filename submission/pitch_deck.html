The problem

Traders repeat the same mistakes. Backtests flatter them.

1. Nobody remembers your mistakes

Every tool starts from zero when you close the tab. The pattern that hurt you last month is invisible at the moment you click buy again.

2. Optimised strategies overfit

Try 400 parameter sets, keep the best, and it looks great on the data you tuned on. Most apps stop there.

The solution

One coach, three jobs

🧪 Test an idea honestly

Type your strategy in plain English or pick one of 4 templates, costs included. Refine on training years; the last year stays locked and grades the result: PASSED or OVERFIT.

📒 Learn from your own trades

Import a broker tradebook. Rex pairs fills into round trips, fingerprints each entry (trend, stretch, volume, volatility, gap) and finds the setups you keep losing in.

🛡️ Check before you click

Symbol, date, side → your most similar past trades → proceed / reduce size / wait, with reasons and sample size. A Challenger argues the other side and can veto.

Why Rex fits “Agents That Remember”

Memory that changes a decision, not a chat log

Memory has a job

Past runs and trades are recalled before the next action and can downgrade proceed → reduce → wait.

Real MCP integration

Built against the live Meko server (42 tools), with the exact argument names it requires (conversation_id, JSON-string metadata). Not a mock.

Evaluated, with a negative result

A walk-forward OFF vs ON test, reported honestly: better drawdown, lower P&L. Memory is judged, not just claimed.
